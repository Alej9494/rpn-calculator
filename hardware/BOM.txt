RPN CALCULATOR - BILL OF MATERIALS
Rev C draft, 2 Nov 2026. Matches the full schematic (rev C).
USB-C is for firmware updates. While plugged in, the LDO powers the calculator
through Q2 and Q1 disconnects the coin cell; otherwise the cell feeds VDD via Q1.
Rev C: five 3.3 V buffers (U6-U10, powered by U5 from 5V_DISP) drive the
panel, which needs a logic high of at least 2.7 V. EXTMODE goes to 5V_DISP.
Parts marked * are candidates: check datasheet and stock before ordering.
Passives are 0805 unless noted. Use 1% resistors throughout.
